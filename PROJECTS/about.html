Our Story

Dress to express,
not to impress.

Born from a love of Korean fashion and Bangkok's vibrant energy.

We believe fashion is
personal expression.

D&G Apparel was founded with a simple idea: clothing should make you feel like yourself — effortless, confident, and free. Inspired by the clean lines of Korean fashion and the vibrant spirit of Bangkok street style, we create pieces that work for real life.

Every piece in our collection is thoughtfully designed with soft, natural fabrics in warm, neutral tones. We believe you should never have to choose between style and comfort.

From the office to a weekend brunch, from a first date to a lazy Sunday — D&G Apparel has your look covered.
